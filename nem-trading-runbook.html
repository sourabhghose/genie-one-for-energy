01 What we're demonstrating

The hero idea: governed, real-time, multi-source intelligence without a pipeline for every question. A trader or analyst asks in plain language; the agent decides whether the answer lives in the live market, in our history, or in AEMO's own analysis — and when a question needs all three, it fetches and reconciles them in one turn.

  • live  "Right now" questions hit a Unity Catalog function that calls AEMO at query time — the price is fetched, not ingested.
  • history  Trends, spikes, averages, fuel mix route to governed UC tables.
  • docs  "Why / what does AEMO say" reads the AEMO reports live from Drive.

The governance story is the subtext throughout: every query runs as the asking user's own identity, through UC permissions and lineage, with in-region Claude. Nothing is copied into a side store.

03 Access — two separate systems

Giving someone the Genie space does not give them the Drive docs, and vice versa. The two legs are governed independently.

databricks  Genie + UC (live + history)

Genie runs queries as the asking user's identity, so each person needs the data grants themselves — space CAN_RUN, USE CATALOG/USE SCHEMA, SELECT on the tables, USE CONNECTION on aemo_live, EXECUTE on the function, and warehouse CAN_USE. Granted to the four non-owner members of #anz-fe-energyutilities:

  • zivile.norkunaite, nauman.shah, allan.leung, beyza.yalavac  — all four fully provisioned. ✓

google drive  Docs (per-user OAuth)

The Drive connector reads as each user's own Google identity — it never proxies the owner's access. So each person must, once:

  1. Have the folder shared to them — done; folder is shared with the team.
  2. Connect Google Drive in OneChat — add the Google Drive source and complete the per-user OAuth with the same @databricks.com Google account the folder was shared to.

Gotchas: the Drive connector is Beta/preview-gated — if someone doesn't see the option, it's a workspace enablement, not self-serve. Docs must be Google-native (ours are Google Docs; PDFs won't read). And the OAuth account must match the one the folder was shared to.

04 Running the demo — sample questions

Grouped by how many sources each exercises. Build from one source to three — the three-source reconciliation is the moment that sells it.

Sources touched: live history docs

Warm-up — single source

Prove each leg works on its own.

  • live"What's the current spot price in SA1?"
  • history"Show the top 5 price spikes in NSW1 this week."
  • docs"Per the AEMO QED Q1 2026 report, what drove wholesale prices and what share did batteries set?"

Two sources — live vs. history

The "two tools, one agent" moment — the agent calls the live API and the tables in a single answer.

  • livehistory"Is SA1's live spot price above or below its 7-day average?"
  • livehistory"Which region has the biggest gap between its live price right now and its 14-day average?"
  • livehistory"Is current demand in VIC1 higher than its typical demand over the last two weeks?"

Three sources — live + history + docs

Grounded in real figures from your docs: renewables hit a 46.5% Q1 high, batteries set prices in 32% of intervals, and SA was the only region with a year-on-year price rise. Ask these in OneChat.

  • livehistorydocs"AEMO's QED Q1 2026 says South Australia was the only NEM region with a year-on-year wholesale price increase. What's the live SA1 price right now, how does it compare to SA1's 14-day average in our data, and does our trend line up with what AEMO says is driving SA prices?"
  • livehistorydocs"The QED report says battery charge and discharge set prices in 32% of intervals, displacing hydro. Does our own generation and price data show batteries shaping prices this week — and what's the live VIC1 price now?"
  • livehistorydocs"AEMO reports renewables hit a record 46.5% of generation in Q1 2026. What's the wind + solar share in our data this week, is the live price low right now as we'd expect, and how does that track the report's narrative?"

Causal — "why did this happen"

Highest-variance, highest-impact. The agent reliably fetches all sources; the narrative quality depends on the model, so rehearse phrasing.

  • livehistorydocs"We saw negative midday prices in SA1 this week. The QED report attributes daytime lows to solar and battery charging — why did it go negative, does our fuel-mix data confirm it, and what's the live SA1 price now that we're past midday?"
  • livehistorydocs"There was a price spike in NSW1 this week. Using the NEM Fact Sheet on how spot prices are set plus the QED price-driver analysis, explain what most likely caused it from our demand and generation data, and tell me whether the live NSW1 price is near that level now."

The hero question

All three sources, plus causal reconciliation, in one turn. Close with this.

  • livehistorydocs"Right now the live SA1 price is one thing, our 14-day data shows another trend, and AEMO's QED Q1 2026 report tells a third story about what's driving SA prices. Reconcile all three: is the current market behaving the way the historical data and AEMO's analysis would predict — and if not, why?"

05 Presenter notes & gotchas

  • Three-source questions only land in OneChat — it combines the Genie agent (live + history) with the Drive docs. The bare Genie space handles live + history only.
  • OneChat auto-routes across every agent you can access — there's no pre-question picker. If a question lands on the wrong agent, use the "Choose another space" control to pin ANZ NEM Trading Intelligence.
  • The live function is single-region per call. Keep "right now" questions to one region (NSW1 / QLD1 / VIC1 / SA1 / TAS1); multi-region live asks may only return one unless the agent iterates.
  • Lead the governance point: the live price is fetched, not stored, and every query runs as the asking user's own identity under UC permissions.
  • Rehearse the "why" questions — they're the highest-variance part of the demo.
